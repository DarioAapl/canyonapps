Privacy Policy

BlockHaven · Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version: BlockHaven collects nothing. It contains no networking code of any kind, so there is nowhere for your data to go even in principle. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The game has no user accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no backend service. It works exactly the same with the device in aeroplane mode.

What stays on your device

The game stores the following locally, in its own private container:

  • Your worlds — the terrain you have changed and everything you have built in it.
  • Any photographs you have hung on poster blocks.
  • The text you have written on signs.
  • Preferences: look sensitivity, movement stick size, left-handed layout, haptics, and day length.

None of this ever leaves your device. Deleting the game removes all of it, and individual worlds can be deleted at any time from the main menu.

Photos

You can hang a picture from your photo library on a wall inside a world. Choosing one uses Apple's system photo picker, which runs outside the game. BlockHaven therefore never requests access to your photo library and never receives any image other than the single one you pick.

That picture is copied into the world's own files on your device. It is not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not shared.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. The game contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service, and no third-party trackers. There are no adverts. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we could not if we wanted to.

Purchases

BlockHaven is free to play. Saving your worlds is unlocked by a single one-time purchase, sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details, your name, or your Apple Account. The game only ever asks Apple a yes-or-no question: has this device bought BlockHaven? Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for any age. It collects no data from anyone, including children. There are no adverts, no links out of the game, no chat, and no way for one player to contact another.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if an optional feature required sending data off-device — we will update this page and the game's App Store privacy details in the same release, and any such feature would be opt-in.
