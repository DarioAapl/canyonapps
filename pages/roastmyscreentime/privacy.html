Privacy Policy

Roast My Screen Time · Last updated 25 August 2026

The short version: Roast My Screen Time collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no backend service to send data to.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally, in your app's private container:

  • Your roast history — the weekly summaries and jokes the app has generated.
  • Achievements you have unlocked.
  • Preferences: your chosen roast personality, haptics setting, and the display name used in Roast Battles.

This data never leaves your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Delete all roast data inside the app.

Screen Time data

Where the app reads device usage, it does so through Apple's Screen Time APIs, which require your explicit permission and process data on-device. The app reads usage totals only — application names and durations. It cannot and does not read your messages, your browsing history, or the content inside any app.

Screen Time data is never transmitted off your device.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service, and no third-party trackers. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Optional premium features are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if an optional feature required sending data off-device — we will update this page and the app's App Store privacy details in the same release, and any such feature would be opt-in.
