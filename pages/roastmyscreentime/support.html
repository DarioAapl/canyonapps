Why does it say "sample data"?

If you declined Screen Time access, or you're on a device where Screen Time isn't available, the app roasts a realistic sample week instead and labels every number as sample data - so you are never shown invented figures as if they were yours. Grant access in iOS Settings › Screen Time and it will use your real usage.

Where do the numbers come from?

Apple's Screen Time APIs, with your permission. The app reads usage totals only: app names, durations, pickup counts and notification counts. It never sees your messages, your browsing, or the content inside any app. Analysis happens entirely on your iPhone and nothing is uploaded.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all roast data. That removes your history and achievements from the device. Deleting the app removes everything too. Nothing is stored anywhere else, because nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore at the top left. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Roast My Screen Time → Cancel. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us.

The roast is wrong / unfair / too mean

Try a different personality in Settings — there are six, and Nice is genuinely nice. If a joke crossed a line, email us and we will remove it. The app is meant to be funny about your habits, never about you.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
